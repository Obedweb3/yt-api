🎬 YouTube Media Downloader API

DataFanatic · youtube-media-downloader · v2

Video

GET/api/info
?url= video ID or YouTube URL  |  &urlAccess=normal|blocked  |  &lang=en-US
GET/api/download
?url= video ID or URL  |  &type=mp4|mp3  |  &quality=1080|720|480… (optional)
GET/api/related
?url= video ID or URL  |  &nextToken= (pagination)  |  &lang=en-US
GET/api/comments
?url= video ID or URL  |  &sortBy=top|newest  |  &nextToken=  |  &lang=en-US

Search

GET/api/search
?q= keyword  |  &searchType=videos|channels|playlists  |  &sortBy=relevance|date|views|rating  |  &duration=short|medium|long  |  &uploadDate=  |  &nextToken=

Playlist

GET/api/playlist
?id= playlist ID  |  &videos=true to list videos  |  &nextToken=  |  &lang=en-US

Channel

GET/api/channel
?id= channel ID or @handle  |  &type=videos|shorts|live|playlists|releases|podcasts  |  &sortBy=newest|popular  |  &nextToken=

Health

GET/api/health
