🎬 YouTube Media Downloader API

Powered by DataFanatic / youtube-media-downloader via RapidAPI.

Video

GET/api/info
?url= YouTube URL or video ID
Get full video details (title, formats, metadata)
GET/api/download
?url= & ?type=mp4|mp3 & ?quality=720 (optional)
Get download URL for MP4 (video) or MP3/M4A (audio). Returns best quality by default.
GET/api/related
?url= & ?nextToken= (optional, for pagination)
List related videos and playlists
GET/api/comments
?url= & ?sortBy=top|newest & ?nextToken=
List video comments with pagination

Search

GET/api/search
?q= keyword & ?type=video|channel|playlist & ?sortBy=relevance|date|views|rating & ?duration=short|long
Search YouTube with filters and pagination

Playlist

GET/api/playlist
?id= Playlist ID & ?nextToken=
Get playlist details and videos

Channel

GET/api/channel
?id= Channel ID & ?nextToken=
Get channel details and videos

Health

GET/api/health
Check API status
